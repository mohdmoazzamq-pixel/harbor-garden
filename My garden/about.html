Major Botanical Classifications in Our Nursery

Understanding how plants grow and bloom helps you design a garden that flourishes across all four seasons.

🌸 Perennial Specimens

Perennials return year after year, deepening their root systems with each season. From majestic Peonies to fragrant English Roses and Astrantia, they form the architectural backbone of any luxury garden estate.

🌿 Biennial Varieties

Biennials complete their lifecycle over two growing seasons — establishing lush foliage in their first year, and exploding into magnificent floral crowns in their second. Foxgloves and Sweet Williams are timeless examples.

🌻 Vibrant Annuals

Annuals pour all their biological energy into non-stop blossom displays throughout a single summer season before setting seed. Sunflowers and Cosmos bring saturated bursts of color to beds and containers.

Harbor Horticultural Best Practices

Essential guidelines developed by our lead arborists to maintain vibrant petal saturation and vigorous root health.

  • 💧
    Deep Morning Irrigation Water early before midday heat to minimize evaporation and prevent fungal mildew on foliage.
  • 🍂
    Organic Pine & Leaf Mulching Maintain a 2-3 inch mulch blanket to regulate root zone temperature and conserve 40% more moisture.
  • ✂️
    Strategic Deadheading Snip faded blossom heads cleanly above leaf nodes to stimulate continuous second-wave blooms.
  • 🔬
    Seasonal Soil Balancing Test soil pH annually. Incorporate vermicompost and rock phosphate to stimulate beneficial microbes.
